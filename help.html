ai-usage

Author: twistezo

ai-usage keeps its own saved Claude and ChatGPT sessions and reads your 5h, weekly and extra usage in the background every minute, pausing after 15 minutes without input. The sessions never leave the app; the panel only receives usage and reset times.

First run

  1. Click the menu bar icon, then “Log in to Claude” or “Log in to ChatGPT”.
  2. Log in with the code from the e-mail. The e-mail link would open in your browser instead of the app.
  3. Close the login window. Usage appears within a few seconds.
  4. Using only one of them? Turn the other off in “Settings”.

Using it

  • “Refresh” reads usage right away instead of waiting for the next minute.
  • “Window” opens the same view as a window you can drag anywhere; pin it to keep it on top.
  • Compact mode shrinks the window to one line and one bar per provider: top half 5h, bottom half the week.
  • Hover a reset to see its exact date and time; in compact mode, hover a value to see when it resets.
  • “Settings” turns providers on or off and lets the app open at login.

Troubleshooting

  • “Session expired or needs verification”: click “Log in to…” and log in again.
  • “Outdated”: no update for over 5 minutes, after a connection problem or while reading is paused for inactivity.
  • “No answer from the site yet”: the site did not respond within 20 seconds; it retries every minute.
