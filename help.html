Help & setup

ai-usage keeps its own saved Claude and ChatGPT sessions and reads your 5h and weekly usage in the background every minute. The sessions never leave the app; the panel only receives usage percentages and reset times.

First run

  1. Click the menu bar icon, then “Log in to Claude” or “Log in to ChatGPT”.
  2. Log in with the code from the e-mail. The e-mail link would open in your browser instead of the app.
  3. Close the login window. Usage appears within a few seconds.

Troubleshooting

  • “Session expired or needs verification”: click “Log in to…” and log in again.
  • “Outdated”: no update has succeeded for a few minutes; check your connection.
  • “Refresh” reads usage right away instead of waiting for the next minute.
